Las Cruces Tree Removal Costs: 2024 Local Price Guide

Published December 2024 | 7 min read

Las Cruces, New Mexico's second-largest city, has unique tree removal pricing influenced by its Chihuahuan Desert location and growing population. If you're planning tree work in Las Cruces, understanding local costs helps you budget and avoid overpaying.

Average Tree Removal Costs in Las Cruces

Tree removal in Las Cruces typically costs $350-$2,400, with most homeowners paying $700-$1,300 for a medium tree. These rates are generally 10-15% lower than Albuquerque due to lower labor costs and cost of living in southern New Mexico.

Small trees (under 20 ft): $350-$600
Medium trees (20-40 ft): $700-$1,300
Large trees (40-60 ft): $1,300-$2,100
Extra large (60+ ft): $2,100-$3,500+

Common Las Cruces Trees and Removal Costs

Desert Willow - $400-$900

Native to southern NM, these smaller ornamental trees are common in Las Cruces landscaping. A typical 15-25 foot desert willow costs $400-$700 to remove.

African Sumac - $600-$1,500

Popular shade tree in Las Cruces. Fast-growing and can reach 30-40 feet. Removal typically runs $800-$1,300 for a mature specimen.

Arizona Ash - $700-$1,800

Common in older Las Cruces neighborhoods. These trees are prone to ash borer issues. Removal costs $700-$1,800 depending on size and condition.

Mexican Palo Verde - $500-$1,200

Native desert tree with distinctive green bark. Smaller size makes removal more affordable at $500-$1,000 for most specimens.

Cottonwood (Bosque Areas) - $1,500-$3,500

Found along the Rio Grande in Las Cruces bosque areas. Large cottonwoods are among the most expensive removals at $1,500-$3,500 due to size.

Why Las Cruces Costs Less Than ABQ

Lower labor rates: Tree service crews in Las Cruces charge $80-$150/hour vs. $100-$200 in Albuquerque.

Cost of living: Las Cruces has a lower cost of living, translating to lower business overhead for contractors.

Less complex removals: Newer developments with better spacing mean fewer trees wedged between structures.

Disposal costs: Dump fees are typically $50-$100 less than in larger NM cities.

Las Cruces Neighborhood Pricing

East Mesa: Standard city pricing. Newer homes with planned landscaping typically have smaller trees. $700-$1,200 average.

Mesilla Valley / Old Mesilla: Mature trees and historic properties. Prices can run 10-15% higher due to care required around older structures.

NMSU Area: Mix of residential and rental properties. Standard pricing with good contractor availability.

Sonoma Ranch: Newer development with younger trees. Often 10-20% below city average due to smaller tree sizes.

Additional Costs in Las Cruces

Stump grinding: $150-$400 (slightly less than northern NM)
Emergency service: +50-100% (monsoon season July-Sept)
Permits: Rarely required for residential private property
Haul-away: $100-$300 if not keeping wood

Best Time for Tree Removal in Las Cruces

Las Cruces' year-round mild weather means tree work can happen any time, but costs vary by season:

Winter (Dec-Feb): Slowest season. Contractors may offer 10-15% discounts. Best time for elective removals.

Spring (Mar-May): Busy season before summer heat. Standard pricing.

Summer (Jun-Aug): Very hot. Some contractors charge slightly more for work in 100°F+ heat, but others offer deals to fill schedules.

Fall (Sep-Nov): Busy season with pleasant weather. Book early or expect 5-10% premium.

Saving Money on Las Cruces Tree Removal

Schedule in winter: Save 10-15% by booking December-February
Keep the firewood: Save $100-$250 in disposal fees
Bundle multiple trees: Second tree typically 15-20% off
Do your own prep: Clear the area yourself, save $75-$150 in labor
Get 3+ quotes: Prices vary $300-$600 for same job
