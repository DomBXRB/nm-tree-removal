Albuquerque Tree Removal Costs: 2024 Price Guide & Local Rates

Published December 2024 | 8 min read

Albuquerque's unique high-desert environment, combined with a mix of native and invasive tree species, creates specific tree removal challenges and costs. If you're planning to remove a tree in ABQ, understanding local pricing will help you budget accurately and avoid overpaying.

In this comprehensive guide, we'll break down exactly what tree removal costs in Albuquerque in 2024, including pricing by tree size, neighborhood variations, and common species-specific rates.

Average Tree Removal Costs in Albuquerque

Tree removal in Albuquerque typically costs between $400 and $2,800, with most homeowners paying around $1,000-$1,500 for a medium-sized tree.

Tree Size Height Range Albuquerque Cost
Small Trees Under 20 feet $400 - $700
Medium Trees 20 - 40 feet $800 - $1,500
Large Trees 40 - 60 feet $1,500 - $2,400
Extra Large Trees Over 60 feet $2,400 - $4,000+

Why Albuquerque Costs What It Does

Several factors make Albuquerque tree removal costs slightly higher than smaller NM cities like Clovis or Roswell, but competitive with other major Southwest metros:

Labor costs: As New Mexico's largest city, ABQ has higher labor rates than rural areas. Tree service crews typically charge $100-$200/hour for labor.

Insurance requirements: Albuquerque contractors face higher insurance costs due to urban density and proximity to expensive properties.

Disposal fees: Dump fees at Albuquerque-area landfills run higher than in smaller cities, adding $75-$150 to typical jobs.

Equipment costs: Urban tree removal often requires specialized equipment (cranes, bucket trucks) to work safely around homes and power lines.

Common Albuquerque Tree Species and Removal Costs

Siberian Elm - $700-$2,200

The most common tree in Albuquerque. These invasive trees grow quickly and often need removal due to weak wood and disease. A typical 30-40 foot Siberian elm costs $1,000-$1,800 to remove in ABQ.

Cottonwood - $1,800-$4,000+

Native along the Rio Grande and North Valley. These massive trees can exceed 80 feet and have extensive root systems. Large cottonwoods are among the most expensive removals in Albuquerque due to their size and the care needed to avoid property damage.

Desert Olive (Russian Olive) - $600-$1,600

Another common invasive species. These thorny trees require extra safety precautions. A 25-35 foot desert olive typically costs $900-$1,400 to remove in Albuquerque.

Mulberry - $600-$1,800

Common in older ABQ neighborhoods. These trees spread aggressively and often need removal when roots damage foundations or sewer lines. Removal costs $600-$1,800 depending on size.

Arizona Cypress - $800-$2,000

Popular landscaping tree in Albuquerque. Dense wood makes them slower to cut than softer trees. Expect to pay $800-$2,000 for a mature cypress removal.

Pricing by Albuquerque Neighborhood

Tree removal costs can vary slightly by neighborhood due to access, property values, and tree density:

North Valley / Los Ranchos - Higher End

Mature trees, larger properties, and proximity to the Rio Grande mean more cottonwoods and complex removals. Expect prices 10-15% above city average. Large cottonwood removals here can exceed $4,000.

Northeast Heights / Foothills - Average to High

Mix of mature landscaping trees and native junipers. Hillside properties can add 15-25% for slope access difficulty. Standard pricing otherwise.

Nob Hill / UNM Area - Average

Older neighborhoods with mature elms and mulberries. Typical city pricing applies. Tight lots may require additional rigging, adding 10-20%.

West Side / Rio Rancho Border - Slightly Lower

Newer developments with smaller, younger trees. Costs typically 5-10% below city average due to easier access and smaller trees.

South Valley - Average

Mix of agricultural and residential areas. More open lots often mean easier access and standard city pricing.

Additional Cost Factors in Albuquerque

Stump Grinding: $150-$600

Most ABQ contractors charge separately for stump grinding. Prices vary based on stump diameter and accessibility.

Emergency Service: +50-150%

Albuquerque's monsoon season (July-September) and occasional winter storms create demand spikes. Emergency tree removal after storm damage can cost 50-150% more than standard rates.

Permit Requirements

Most residential tree removal in Albuquerque doesn't require permits UNLESS:

  • Tree is on city property or easement
  • Tree is in Old Town historic district
  • Tree is a designated heritage tree (rare)

When permits are required, expect to pay $50-$150 for the permit plus any contractor administrative fees.

How to Save Money on Tree Removal in Albuquerque

Schedule in winter: December through February is the slowest season for tree work in ABQ. Many contractors offer 10-20% discounts during these months.

Bundle multiple trees: If you have several trees to remove, doing them all at once typically saves $150-$200 per additional tree.

Keep the firewood: If you can use or give away the wood, you'll save $150-$300 in haul-away fees. Cottonwood and elm make decent firewood for Albuquerque's mild winters.

Skip the stump initially: If the stump isn't in your way, defer grinding for 6-12 months and do it separately when budget allows.

Get multiple quotes: Prices can vary by $400-$800 for the same job between different ABQ contractors. Always get at least 3 quotes.

Questions to Ask Albuquerque Tree Removal Companies

Before hiring an ABQ tree service, ask:

  • "Are you licensed and insured for tree work in Albuquerque?" (Verify with NM RLD)
  • "Does your quote include stump grinding, or is that extra?"
  • "What's included in cleanup? Will you rake and remove all debris?"
  • "Do we need any permits from the City of Albuquerque?"
  • "Is disposal/haul-away included in this price?"
  • "Do you have references from recent ABQ jobs I can contact?"
